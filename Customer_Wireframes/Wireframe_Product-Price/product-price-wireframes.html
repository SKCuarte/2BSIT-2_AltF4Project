Destructive actions require confirmation. Delete/restore terminology must distinguish product deactivation from price-row deletion.

Deletion semanticsMigration 001 gives PRICEHIST both Status and stamp. No explicit delete rule states whether status becomes Inactive or the row is physically removed.

Open questions

  1. Which right or role rule grants the “View” action: is there a separate Product view right, or can access to the module imply View?
  2. How is Restore authorized: Delete Product, a new restore right, or server/manage policy?
  3. Does opening Price History require a separate View Price right, or any of Add/Edit/Delete Price?
  4. Is effective-date editing allowed; or should Edit keep the date read-only because it is part of the composite primary key?
  5. Does deleting a price row hard-delete it or set status Inactive? What happens to the next applicable price?
  6. Does Add Product create the first price row, or is Price History the only price-entry workflow?
  7. Does current price resolve by CURRENT_DATE or allow a selected date in the UI?
  8. Should unit_name be a separate lookup table, or remain the database CHECK values shown here?
  9. Which search scope and filters are canonical? Issue #93 mentions category/unit filtering; the schema has unit but no category column.
  10. How should P-004-style missing prices be labeled, and is $0.00 valid or reserved for an intentionally free item?
